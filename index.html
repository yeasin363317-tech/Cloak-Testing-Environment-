Privacy Policy

Cloak doesn't require sign-up and doesn't store any personal information beyond what you type into a room. No accounts, no emails, no phone numbers — just a name you choose and a random session token kept only on your own device.

Messages exist only while a room is open. Everything — messages, member list, and the room record itself — lives only as long as the room does. When the room's creator closes it, all of that is permanently deleted from the database. Not archived. Not hidden. Actually gone.

Cloak does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising of any kind.

Terms of Service

Cloak is provided as-is, for casual and private conversations between people who trust each other with a room code.

Don't use Cloak for anything illegal or intended to harm others. Rooms and their contents are temporary by design — they can be lost due to a network issue, a member closing the room, or ordinary technical problems. Don't rely on Cloak for anything you need to keep permanently.

Cloak is offered without warranty of any kind. Use it at your own discretion.
